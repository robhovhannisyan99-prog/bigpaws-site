One line

A warm polar bear adventure where the ice cracks under every step and the fish can hear you coming.

Description

In Big Paws you play a polar bear cub on its first spring out of the den. Mom teaches you to read the ice: thick white ice holds you, dark thin ice and see-through glass ice break fast, and if you stand still too long you go splash. Fish hear your footsteps through the ice, so you learn to walk softly, wait, and leap at the right moment. The first ten-level test is in preparation, with no ads or purchases in that test.

Features

  • Living sea ice that cracks, sags and breaks under your weight
  • Fish that hear you: sneak, wait and leap
  • Charged jumps across the tide crack, belly slides over glass ice
  • A first chapter of lessons from Mom, from cracking ice to a reunion in the fog
  • No sign-in or chat; no ads or purchases in the first test
