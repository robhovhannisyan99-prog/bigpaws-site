A polar bear game where the ice is alive

The ice cracks under your paws. Stand still too long and you go splash. Thick white ice holds you; dark thin ice and see-through glass ice break fast. And under it, the fish can hear every step you take.

The ice cracks under every step

Thick white ice holds you. Dark thin ice cracks fast, so keep moving and run for the snow.

Sneak up on the fish

Footsteps send rings through the ice. Walk soft, wait for the moment, then pounce.

Leap for your dinner

Fish rise to the light of the ice holes. Hold to charge a jump and catch one mid-air.

Bring it home to Mom

Carry your catch to Mom's basket, or toss it to her when it's too heavy to jump with.

Too loud? They swim away!

Stomp across the ice and the fish dart off. Sneak, or walk on the snow.

Follow Mom through the fog

In the last lesson Mom goes on ahead. Sniff out her scent and find her in the fog.

For parents

Big Paws is a family adventure made by an independent developer. The first chapter teaches the cub to read the ice, catch fish and find the way back to Mom.

  • Free first test. No ads or purchases in this test.
  • No sign-in or chat. Progress is saved on your device.
  • Plays offline. No internet connection needed.
  • Short levels of 2 to 5 minutes, with checkpoints along the way.
  • Gentle humor. Splashes, tumbles and a fish to the face. Nobody ever gets hurt.
