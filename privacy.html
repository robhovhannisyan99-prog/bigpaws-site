Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Big Paws ("the game") is made by Robert, an independent developer ("I", "me"). The game is made for everyone, including children and families. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information. It applies to the Big Paws app on Google Play (package com.rob.bigpaws) and to this website.

The short version

Big Paws does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no tracking, and it works offline.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (such as levels completed, stars and settings) on your own device. The game does not send it to me or to anyone else, and I cannot see it. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include it in your own Google backup, which belongs to your Google account. Uninstalling the game deletes the save from the device.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location or contacts.
  • It does not show ads and does not use the advertising ID or any advertising or analytics services.
  • It does not let players talk to or share content with other people.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

The game engine

Big Paws is made with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics and diagnostics reporting are switched off in the game, so it does not send usage or crash data to Unity.

Google Play

You download the game from Google Play, which is run by Google. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example crash reports and install statistics that it shows developers only as anonymous totals. I cannot identify any player from them.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Links that leave the game, such as this policy, are behind a grown-up check. The game is made to meet Google Play's Families policy.

If you contact me

If you email me, I see your email address and your message. I use them only to answer you, never share them, and delete them when they are no longer needed. Children should ask a parent or guardian to write for them.

This website

This website has no cookies, forms, ads or analytics. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Changes

If a future version of the game changes any of this, I will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: bigpaws.support@gmail.com
